Eight free spreadsheet tools for one-person businesses

Most small businesses do not need new software to see their numbers. They need one sheet that answers one question, filled in a few minutes a week. These eight are free (pay what you want, $0 is fine). Each opens in Excel, Numbers or LibreOffice and imports to Google Sheets, comes with sample data so you can see it working first, and has a Start here tab that walks you through setup.

For freelancers

  • Free Invoice Tracker Lite: log each invoice once and see what is unpaid, how many days late it is and which clients pay slowest. When you also want tax set-aside and cash runway, the paid version is Freelancer Finance OS ($19).
  • Free Time Tracker Lite: log hours for one client and up to five projects and see what each project pays you per hour against your target rate. The Freelance Time & Project Profitability Tracker ($12) covers 30 clients and 60 projects.

For creators and newsletter writers

  • Free Sponsorship Rate Calculator: type your views, opens or downloads and get suggested sponsorship rates plus a one-page rate card to send to brands. The Sponsorship Rate Card & Deal Tracker ($15) adds a deal pipeline, and the Newsletter Dashboard ($12) tracks open rate, paid churn and sponsor revenue.

For landlords and hosts

  • Free Rent Tracker Lite: a rent roll for one property with up to 5 units: who has paid, who is behind, which leases end soon and what the property nets this year. The Rental Property Tracker ($19) handles up to 40 units across several properties, with deposits.
  • Free Airbnb Host Calculator: log each stay once and see occupancy, average nightly rate and net profit by month. The Airbnb Host Spreadsheet ($19) adds per-listing profit and payout checks.

For job seekers

  • Free Job Search Tracker Lite: up to 40 applications with five stage dates; it flags who to follow up with and shows your response rate by source. The Job Search Command Center ($12) adds 150 rows, an offer comparison and interview prep.

For makers and SaaS founders

  • Free Launch Countdown Checklist: type your launch date and 50 launch tasks get due dates, with overdue and due-soon flags. The Product Launch Planner ($9) adds a timed launch-day runbook and results by channel.
  • Free SaaS Metrics Dashboard Lite: type a few numbers a month and get MRR, ARR, churn and quick ratio. The SaaS Metrics Dashboard ($19) adds CAC, LTV, payback and runway.

How to pick one

Start with the question that costs you the most when you get it wrong. If a client is late, start with invoices. If you are not sure a project was worth it, start with time. Fill it for two weeks before adding anything else; a sheet you keep up beats a bigger one you abandon.
